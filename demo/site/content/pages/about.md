# About

I am a distributed systems engineer in Porto. I have spent about a decade on
systems where the interesting problems are not the algorithms but the seams:
retries, ordering, partial failure, and what the operator sees at three in the
morning.

Currently staff engineer at Ledgerbeam, working on event-sourced inventory and
the ingestion pipelines that feed it. Before that, six years across payments
and logistics, which is where I learned that most "performance problems" are
policy problems in a costume.

## What this site is

A working notebook. Posts here are written when something surprised me and I
had to understand it properly — which means they are uneven in length, occasionally
wrong, and usually specific.

I write about the BEAM because that is where I work, but the recurring subject
is narrower than that: what a system does when it cannot keep its promises, and
how you find out.
